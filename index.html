01/12Gulf of Tunis

Carthage

The city of Dido, then of Rome.

Founded by Phoenicians, destroyed by Rome in 146 BC and rebuilt as a Roman capital. The Antonine Baths stand right on the sea.

UNESCO World Heritage, 1979

02/12Gulf of Tunis

Sidi Bou Said

Blue doors, white walls, open sea.

A clifftop village above the Gulf of Tunis, named after the Sufi saint Abu Said al-Baji. Mint tea with a view of the bay.

03/12Gulf of Tunis

La Marsa

Where Tunis goes to the sea.

A seaside town just north of Carthage, with a long beach and a corniche made for evening walks.

04/12The far north

Bizerte

An old port at the top of Africa.

Colourful houses around the old harbour and kasbah, and wide beaches close to the northernmost tip of Africa.

05/12Kroumirie mountains

Aïn Draham

Snow in Tunisia? Yes.

A mountain town in cork oak forests near the Algerian border, and one of the few places in Tunisia where snow falls in winter.

06/12The northwest

Dougga

A whole Roman town on a hill.

Temples, a theatre and the Capitol, in what UNESCO calls the best-preserved Roman small town in North Africa.

UNESCO World Heritage, 1997

07/12Central Tunisia

Kairouan

The first Islamic city of the Maghreb.

Founded in 670. Its Great Mosque is one of the oldest in North Africa, and its medina is known for carpets and makroudh pastries.

UNESCO World Heritage, 1988

08/12Cap Bon coast

Hammamet

A walled medina on the beach.

Long sandy beaches, jasmine in summer, and an old medina whose walls stand right at the edge of the sand.

09/12The Sahel

El Jem

A colosseum among the olive groves.

Built in the 3rd century for around 35,000 spectators, it is one of the largest amphitheatres of the Roman world.

UNESCO World Heritage, 1979

10/12The south

Matmata

Luke Skywalker grew up here.

Berber homes dug into the ground around open courtyards. One of them, Hotel Sidi Driss, was the Lars homestead in Star Wars (1977).

11/12Gateway to the Sahara

Douz

Where the Sahara begins.

Camel rides into the dunes of the Grand Erg Oriental, and every winter the International Festival of the Sahara.

12/12Oases of the Djerid

Tozeur

A salt lake that holds the sky.

Palm-grove oases, an old town of patterned brick, and Chott el Jerid, a vast salt flat known for its mirages.
